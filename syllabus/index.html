Course Description

This is a capstone course in which students will have the opportunity to demonstrate their competences to integrate and apply information systems (is) knowledge and skills acquired through their is program. with the approval of the course instructor, students will work in teams on a business problem or opportunity, develop, test, implement, document, and demonstrate an innovative information technology (it)-based solution. Throughout the project life cycle, students will demonstrate their proficiency in selecting and utilizing appropriate methodologies, techniques, and tools to carry out the tasks required to develop and manage their projects.

Course Learning Objectives (CLOs)

Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

  1. apply skills acquired from prior courses in information system
  2. build a fully functional information system
  3. learn team management and problem solving skills
  4. acquire practical project management skills
  5. build and improve system analysis and design skills
  6. build and improve communication skills
  7. build and improve programming skills
  8. build and improve business analytical skills

Appendix: CBA Vision, Mission and Program Learning Goals

CBA Vision. To be the leading provider of quality business education in the region.

CBA Mission. As part of Kuwait University, the leading national institution of higher education, the College of Business Administration is committed to providing quality business education, engaging in research and community services to contribute to the socio-economic development of the country.

CBA Program Learning Goals (Undergraduate, 2025-2029)

PLG1 - Analytical Competency. A CBA graduate will be able to use analytical skills to solve business problems and make a well-supported business decision.

  1. Use appropriate analytical techniques to solve a given business problem.
  2. Integrate innovative and critical thinking in the analysis and resolution of business challenges.
  3. Critically evaluate multiple solutions to a business problem.
  4. Make well-supported business decisions.

PLG2 - Oral Communication Competency. A CBA graduate will be able to communicate effectively in a wide variety of business settings.

  1. Deliver clear, concise, and audience-centered presentations.

PLG3 - Written Communication Competency. A CBA graduate will be able to communicate effectively in writing in a wide variety of business settings.

  1. Write clear, concise, and audience-centered business documents.

PLG4 - Information Technology Competency. A CBA graduate will be able to utilize Information Technology for the completion of business tasks.

  1. Use data-processing tools to analyze or solve business problems.
  2. Apply information technology tools and techniques creatively to support innovation in business solutions.

PLG5 - Ethical Competency. A CBA graduate will be able to recognize ethical issues present in the business environment, analyze the tradeoffs between different ethical perspectives, and make a well-supported ethical decision.

  1. Identify the ethical dimensions of a business decision.
  2. Recognize and analyze the ethical tradeoffs created by balancing interests of multiple stakeholders.
  3. Evaluate the ethical implications of business decisions including any business and social sustainability implications.
  4. Formulate and defend a well-supported recommendation for the resolution of an ethical issue.

PLG6 - General Business Knowledge. A CBA graduate will be able to demonstrate a basic understanding of the main business disciplines' concepts and theories.

  1. Acquire a fundamental understanding of knowledge from the main business disciplines (e.g. finance, accounting, marketing, and management information systems, among others).
