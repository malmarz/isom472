The first class

Who does what, and one story from start to finish, then team work for the rest of the period. Quiz 2 runs at the end of the class.

Reading

Required, before Wednesday 7 October. Agent safety and the sandbox — why the tool you build with is allowed to touch some things and not others, one documented case, what the Antigravity sandbox does, and why you still own what shipped — is no longer a lecture. It is a reading, and it replaces the lecture on the same topic.

  • Reading — Agent safety and the sandbox

Before the second class

  • Finish Phase 2 and tag it in the repository. The tag is what gets graded.
  • Have your system reachable at its public address. You present from that address, not from a laptop.
  • Prepare your own questions for the other teams. Arriving without them is itself a failure of the session.

The second class

The Phase 2 delivery. There are no slides for this session and it is not a queue of private meetings: all teams are in the room, each team presents, and the rest of the class is the audience and the question pool. You take questions from the room, and you ask them. Every delivery is presented from a public address.

Due this week

Phase 2 — stories, the schema, the split, the environment, and your token plan. 10 marks. The delivery contains:

  • A backlog at or above the floor, ordered, with acceptance criteria.
  • Acceptance criteria that say what one member's work hands to another's, using the column names in the schema.
  • The split — who holds which named stories this sprint.
  • The schema, with keys marked and row-level security enabled on every table.
  • A verified environment, working for every member.
  • The token plan, and the first ledger entry.

The phase is tagged in the repository at its due date, and the tag is what gets graded — not a submitted document and not a slide deck. See your repository for the tag names and the rules, and deliverables for the date and the floor.

Use

  • Your repository — the tag names, the layout, and the pull request rules.
  • Deliverables — the Phase 2 date, weight and checklist.
  • Team roles — who answers for which part of the delivery.
