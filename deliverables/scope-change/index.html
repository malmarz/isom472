Scope change

Adding a story outside your proposal: the four steps.

ISOM 472 › Deliverables › Scope change

A story outside §6 of your proposal counts only if you do all four steps.

The four steps #

  1. Ask the client first, and get the answer in writing (a message, an email, or meeting notes they confirm).
  2. Add a “Scope change” section to the issue: who asked or agreed, and the date; which persona it helps and what problem it solves; the link to the client's written answer.
  3. Say what it replaces. Name the story it pushes down or out. If nothing moves, say why you still have room.
  4. Log it in docs/scope-changes.md, one row per story: Date | Issue | Client evidence (link) | Replaces. If the file is not in your repo, create it.

Without all four, the story is out of scope. It is read in row D of the sprint rubric.
