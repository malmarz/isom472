Basket Register — Phase 2 delivery note (a team of five)

1. What was decided. Volunteers at the Jahra branch register families, record a monthly basket collection, and are stopped from recording a second collection for the same family in the same month. Two personas: the counter volunteer and the branch coordinator. Four tables: families, collections, volunteers, areas. RLS: volunteers read and insert; only the coordinator updates or deletes. The coordinator's monthly count is read-only in Phase 3.
